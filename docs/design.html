Game Design Document

Grave Silence — Aragami 2-style stealth in a zombie apocalypse.

Vision

Grave Silence captures the feel of Aragami 2 — fluid movement, shadow-based supernatural powers, and rewarding ghost play — transplanted into a zombie apocalypse. The player is an "Umbral Walker," someone who can slip between shadows while the dead hunt by sight and sound.

Design Pillars

  • Power fantasy through stealth — Feel like a predator, not a survivor with a gun.
  • Readable enemy behavior — Zombies telegraph awareness clearly.
  • Meaningful darkness — Light and shadow are gameplay mechanics.
  • Consequence for mistakes — Alerting the horde is recoverable but costly.

Umbral Powers

Umbral Step (Q)

Teleport to a shadow zone within range. Cost: 20 energy · Cooldown: 3s · Range: 12m

Umbral Cloak (F)

Become invisible in shadow zones. Cost: 35 energy · Cooldown: 15s · Duration: 5s

Umbral Lure (R)

Place a decoy that emits noise. Cost: 25 energy · Cooldown: 10s · Duration: 8s

Umbral Strike (Right Mouse)

Instant stealth kill on unaware zombies. Cost: 15 energy · Range: 2.5m

Enemy Awareness

Patrol → Investigate (heard noise) → Chase → Attack. Awareness decays over time when the player is hidden.

Detection Inputs

  • Sight — Cone-based, modified by player visibility
  • Hearing — Footsteps, traps, lures, screams
  • Alert propagation — Nearby zombies are alerted when one detects the player

Level Design Guidelines

  • Every encounter should have at least 2 solutions: bypass or eliminate
  • Shadow highways — connected dark routes through the level
  • Verticality — rooftops, fire escapes, broken floors
  • Screamers placed to punish direct routes

Reference Games

  • Aragami 2 — Core stealth loop, shadow powers, mission structure
  • The Last of Us — Zombie threat, environmental storytelling
  • Dishonored — Multiple approaches, awareness system
  • Mark of the Ninja — Clarity of stealth feedback
