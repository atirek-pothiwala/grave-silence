Umbral Walker

Grave Silence

Move through ruined cities in the shadows. Use umbral powers to eliminate the undead silently — an Aragami 2-style stealth game in a zombie world. Low-poly, flat-shaded visuals.

Low-Poly Art Style

Flat Shaded

Faceted meshes, no smooth normals. Every face catches hard moonlight.

Solid Colors

No textures. Umbral purples, zombie greens, concrete grays.

Blocky Silhouettes

Cube-built characters and buildings. Readable, fast, stylized.

Low Vertex Budget

Under 500 tris per character. Built for clarity, not photorealism.

New in Latest Build

  • Spirit Vision Tab to reveal zombie awareness through walls.
  • Mission Scoring Ghost, Silent, and Speed medals tracked automatically.

  • Zombie Brute Heavy enemy that cannot be stealth-killed.

The Dead

  • Shambler Slow patrol, low awareness. Easy to sneak past.
  • Runner Fast chase once alerted. Stick to shadow routes.
  • Screamer Alerts the entire area on detection. Kill first.
  • Brute Cannot be stealth-killed. Avoid or distract.
